Repo rhew/rhew.org conventions: commit subjects are short sentence-case summaries, usually no Conventional Commit prefixes, no terminal period, and often direct content descriptions like “Add Cody memorial post and related pet tags”, “Fix links”, or “Update tags and date”. Hugo content lives under hugo-src/content/posts/<slug>/index.md with front matter title, date, summary, tags, optional cover, and optional lastmod only when changed after publication. Site voice varies by tag: recipes are practical and structured, rhew.org/code posts are first-person, plainspoken, terminal/static-site focused, supervillainy posts use playful retired-overlord/minion language, and memorial/pet posts use restrained, sincere prose. Preserve James’s voice over making the prose bland.

James-developed local Hermes skills live under `/opt/data/skills/james/<short-name>/` to distinguish them from bundled, hub-installed, or upstream Hermes skills in their existing category paths. Frontmatter skill names stay descriptive and do not need a `james-` prefix when the path shows ownership.
